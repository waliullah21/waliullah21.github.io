About

Applied math, applied to real problems

A little about who I am and what drives my work.

I'm a PhD candidate in Applied Mathematics at UC Merced, where I work at the intersection of statistics, machine learning, and mathematical modeling. My research turns time‑series and biological data into models that help explain public‑health and biological phenomena — from climate‑linked disease dynamics to early markers of chronic illness.

Alongside research, I teach at the university level and enjoy mentoring students through the sometimes intimidating, often beautiful, world of applied math. When I'm not deriving equations or training models, I like building clean, fast web interfaces — this site included.
