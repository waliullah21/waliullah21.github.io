About

Applied math, applied to real problems

A little about who I am and what drives my work.

I'm a PhD candidate in Applied Mathematics at UC Merced, advised by Prof. Dr. Roummel Marcia, where I work at the intersection of statistics, machine learning, and mathematical modeling. My research turns time‑series and biological data into models that help explain public‑health and biological phenomena — from climate‑linked disease dynamics to epidemiological forecasting of Valley Fever in California.

Alongside research, I teach at the university level and enjoy mentoring students through the sometimes intimidating, often beautiful, world of applied math. I hold an M.S. and B.S. in Applied Mathematics from Noakhali Science and Technology University, Bangladesh, and have contributed to peer‑reviewed work spanning epidemiology, hydrology, agriculture, and renewable energy forecasting.
